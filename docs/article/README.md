# Shusha article (AzTU "Elmi əsərlər" format)

`shusha_meqale.docx` follows the author instructions of the AzTU journal
"Elmi əsərlər" (proceedings.aztu.edu.az/instructions) and the layout of its
published 2026 issue: A4, 2 cm margins, Times New Roman 12 pt, single
spacing, 1 cm indent, Azerbaijani + English title/author/abstract blocks,
run-in section headings, ƏDƏBİYYAT, Azerbaijani Xülasə at the end.

Before submitting, fill the bracketed fields: patronymic, workplace,
e-mail, ORCID. The running header's issue/pages/DOI are assigned by the
editorial office.

Rebuild: `python3 figures.py && node build_article.js`
Benchmark numbers in Table 2 come from `tests/crypto_bench.c`.
